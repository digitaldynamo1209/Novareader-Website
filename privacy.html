Security & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026 • Version 2.0

The NovaReader Promise: NovaReader is built on a strict 100% Offline, Privacy-First philosophy. We do not track you, we do not require account registration, and your personal reading files never leave your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional e-reader platforms, NovaReader operates without cloud tracking. Specifically:

  • No Account Information: You are never asked for your name, email, phone number, password, or payment information.
  • No Reading Telemetry: We do not track what books you own, what titles you read, your reading speed, your highlights, or when you open a file.
  • No Cloud Document Uploads: Your books, comics, notes, and PDFs remain strictly on your local computer or phone. NovaReader does not transmit your files to any remote server or cloud service.
  • No Ad Networks or Profiling: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or user profiling cookies.

2. How Device Permissions & Local Storage Work

A. Windows Desktop Version

  • Folder Monitoring: NovaReader accesses only the local directories you explicitly select (e.g., Downloads, Documents, or custom book folders). Scanning is performed entirely locally on your processor.
  • Local Settings Storage: Your library settings, format preferences, and excluded book lists are saved in a local configuration file on your PC (%USERPROFILE%\.novareader\). This data is never sent outside your machine.

B. Android Mobile & Tablet Version

  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): On Android, NovaReader uses Google's scoped Storage Access Framework. You choose the specific book folders the app is permitted to read. NovaReader does not request or possess broad device-wide access.
  • Permissions Persistence: Permission grants are saved solely in Android's secure app sandbox to preserve your library across device restarts.

3. File Integrity & Safety

NovaReader is designed as a read-only bookshelf catalog and launcher:

  • NovaReader never modifies, renames, alters, moves, or deletes your original book files.
  • When you uncheck a book in the Discovery Checklist, NovaReader simply hides it from your bookshelf display—it never deletes the file from your disk.

4. Third-Party Reader Application Handoff

NovaReader acts as your physical library organizer. When you click or tap a book to read it, NovaReader instructs your operating system to launch your default reading application (such as Adobe Acrobat, SumatraPDF, Calibre, Kindle for PC, ReadEra, or Moon+ Reader) via standard local OS intents.

These external applications are governed by their own respective privacy policies and developer terms. NovaReader does not transmit any information to them beyond requesting the OS open the specified local file.

5. Website & Software Updates

  • Website Hosting: The NovaReader download website is hosted via Netlify. Like standard web servers, Netlify may record standard anonymous web server logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agents, and timestamped requests) strictly for network security and content delivery.
  • Update Checks: When checking for software updates, NovaReader retrieves an anonymous, static version.json file from the server. No device identifiers, user IDs, or library contents are transmitted with this request.

6. Children's Privacy

NovaReader does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for family, academic, and private use.

7. Updates to This Policy

If we update our privacy practices or release new platform features, any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated "Effective Date".

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or NovaReader's data practices, please reach out via our official website support links at NovaReader Support.
